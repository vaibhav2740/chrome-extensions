Pawnshot

Chess board scanner and analysis board ·

Pawnshot is installed

  1. Pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar and pin Pawnshot, so it is one click away.
  2. Open it on any page. Click the Pawnshot icon, or press Alt+Shift+A. Click again to hide it.
  3. Press Snapshot. It reads the board from chess.com and lichess, from a YouTube (or any) video, or from the screen, and shows the best move, the evaluation and the main lines.
  4. Play through ideas. Drag pieces on the panel board, step back and forth with ← and →, or open the position on Lichess or Chess.com.

Pawnshot switches itself off while a game is being played on chess.com or lichess. It is made for learning: videos, books, reviews and your finished games.

Fair play

Using an engine in a game that is being played is cheating, and chess.com and lichess close accounts for it. Pawnshot locks itself on chess.com, chesskid.com and lichess while a game or a rated puzzle is in progress on the tab, and every way of reading the board (the page, a video, a screenshot) is refused there. It unlocks when the game is over.

Privacy

Pawnshot does not collect, store or send anything about you or the pages you visit. Pictures of boards are read on your device and are never uploaded. Settings stay in your browser.

If you buy Premium, your license key is checked with the shop that sold it (about once a week). Nothing else is sent. Privacy policy

About

Pawnshot is free software (GPL-3.0). It uses Stockfish (GPL-3.0), the chessimg2pos board reader (MIT), ONNX Runtime Web (MIT) and the cburnett pieces. Source code: github.com/vaibhav2740/chrome-extensions.
